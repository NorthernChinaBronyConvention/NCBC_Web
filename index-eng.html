Venue

VENUE

Flamingo Harbour Hotel Tianjin

Address: No. 1 Renai East Road, Jinghai District, Tianjin

Welcome to the Flamingo Harbour Hotel, where you will embark on a dream-chasing journey that perfectly blends natural charm with comfortable accommodation. The hotel features an elegant flamingo theme, infusing every detail with a touch of romantic crimson vitality.
This is not just a place to stay, but a harbor to connect with nature. Adjacent to the venue, the Guanghe Valley is built by the lake, offering all pony fans a wonderful natural experience. The hotel also features entertainment and exploration spaces where laughter echoes in every corner. Exquisite cuisine and attentive service are the warmest comfort during your journey.
We sincerely hope that during this DreamLandCon celebration, the Flamingo Harbour Hotel will weave beautiful memories of dancing with nature for you. We look forward to your visit to begin this deep blue dream-chasing journey.

THEME & VISION

DreamLandCon

We firmly believe that the fan community is an important reason why MLP: FiM continues to shine years after its conclusion. Therefore, maintaining and promoting this fan spirit is our long-term goal.
Following the 2025 NCBC with FOE (Fallout: Equestria) as the theme, we have found a new realm for 2026 DLC - LAYU (Luna's Academy for Young Unicorns) as our platform to look toward the future, sharing stories of ponies and their fan works with all of you.
